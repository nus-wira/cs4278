\subsection{N-Dimensional Disc}
Rejection sampling can be used where $\forall i \in \{1,2,\dots,N\}$,
sample $x_i\in[0,r]$, where $p=(x_1,x_2,\dots,x_n)$.
Then for disc with center $c$, reject any $p$ where $||p-c||>r$.
Given the volume of an $n$-dimensional disc with radius $R$ is
\[V_N(R)=\frac{\pi^{n/2}}{\Gamma\left(\frac{N}{2}+1\right)}R^N\]
where $\Gamma$ is Leonhard Euler's gamma function
\[\Gamma(n+\frac{1}{2})
=(n-\frac{1}{2})\cdot(n-\frac{3}{2})\cdot\dots\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\pi^{1/2}\]
We have the rejection rate
\[\frac{r^N-V_N(r)}{r^N}=1-\frac{\pi^{N/2}}{\Gamma\left(\frac{N}{2}+1\right)}\]
Since $\pi^N$ increases much more quickly than $\Gamma(N)$,
we have the rejection rate quickly diminishing as $N$ increases.
